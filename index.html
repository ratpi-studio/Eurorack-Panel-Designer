Eurorack Panel Designer

A free, open source editor for Eurorack front panels, running entirely in your browser. Place real parts at the hole their datasheet calls for, see the panel in 3D as it will be printed, and export STL, SVG, PNG or KiCad Edge.Cuts. No account, no upload.

  • Formats: 3U Eurorack (128.5 mm), 1U Intellijel (39.65 mm), 1U Pulp Logic tiles (43.18 mm), 2U, 4U, or a custom size.
  • Widths in HP or millimeters, cut at the widths Doepfer publishes: 6 HP is 30.00 mm, not 30.48 mm.
  • Thonkiconn jacks, Alpha 9 mm pots, Bourns PEC11R encoders, Dailywell toggles and LEDs, with their knobs, nuts and washers outlined.
  • Mounting holes generated on the 5.08 mm grid of the rails.
  • Text in five bundled fonts and SVG patterns, raised for a two-colour 3D print.

Reference tables: HP to mm · panel dimensions · drill sizes · 3D printing · KiCad export
